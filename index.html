Research Identity

Strategic Cognitive Offloading in AI-Mediated Learning

Exploring how learners strategically distribute cognitive effort between humans and AI while preserving agency and supporting higher-order thinking.

My research focuses on AI-mediated learning mechanisms across argumentative dialogue, multimodal environments, collective intelligence, emotional regulation, and language learning. I study how human–AI interaction shapes reasoning, transfer, and knowledge construction through design-based, experimental, and interaction-analytic approaches.

About

I investigate how generative AI can function as a cognitive collaborator rather than a mere tool. My work connects theory, learning mechanisms, and instructional design to explain when AI supports thinking and when it risks cognitive passivity.
