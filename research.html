Research Program

Strategic Cognitive Offloading Architecture

This research program examines how cognitive work should be distributed between learners and AI, how interaction mechanisms operate, and how those mechanisms are realized in specific learning domains.

Research constellation connecting argumentation, emotional regulation, multimodal learning, collective intelligence, and language learning under a shared AI-mediated learning agenda.

Core Theory

Strategic Cognitive Offloading explains how learners can distribute cognitive effort between themselves and AI in ways that preserve agency while supporting higher-order thinking.

Mechanism Focus

I analyze how dialogue, sequencing, scaffolding, multimodal interaction, and collaborative knowledge building with AI shape reasoning, transfer, and knowledge construction.

Domain Applications

The agenda extends across argumentative writing, AI-supported project-based learning, collective intelligence, emotional regulation, and elementary language learning.

Three-layer framework connecting theory, AI-mediated learning mechanisms, and data-driven evidence.

How the Program Is Organized

  • Theory: Strategic Cognitive Offloading
  • Mechanisms: dialogue, sequencing, scaffolding, transfer, collective interaction
  • Evidence: design-based research, experiments, sequence analysis, log data, and multimethod learning analytics
