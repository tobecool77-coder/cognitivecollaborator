Research Program

AI-Mediated Learning Architecture

My research investigates how generative AI acts as a cognitive collaborator in learning environments. The agenda is organized around strategic cognitive offloading, human–AI interaction mechanisms, and domain-specific applications in collaborative learning.

Research constellation linking argumentation, emotional regulation, multimodal learning, collective intelligence, and language learning under a shared AI-mediated learning agenda.

Core Theory

Strategic Cognitive Offloading explores how cognitive work should be distributed between learners and AI in ways that preserve agency while supporting higher-order thinking.

Mechanism Focus

I analyze how dialogue, sequencing, and collaborative interaction with AI shape reasoning, transfer, and knowledge construction.

Three-layer framework connecting theory, AI-mediated learning mechanisms, and data-driven evidence.
