Research Program

Strategic Cognitive Offloading Architecture

Examining how cognitive work should be distributed between learners and AI, how interaction mechanisms operate, and how those mechanisms are realized across learning domains.

Core Theory

Strategic Cognitive Offloading

How learners distribute cognitive effort between themselves and AI in ways that preserve agency while supporting higher-order thinking. The key distinction is between offloading that frees working memory for deeper reasoning versus offloading that bypasses thinking entirely.

Domain Applications
  • 💬 AI argumentation & writing transfer
  • 🎨 Creativity & project-based learning
  • 🤝 Human–AI collective intelligence
  • 🌱 Emotional regulation & schema transfer
  • 🗣️ Elementary language learning
  • 📐 Geometry · AR · Embodied interaction
