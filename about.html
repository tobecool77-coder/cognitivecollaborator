Profile

I am a developmental researcher investigating how structured learning environments shape cognitive and motivational growth in children. My research examines how AI-mediated environments can scaffold executive functioning, self-regulation, and higher-order reasoning while preserving learner agency.

Drawing on both longitudinal classroom research and experimental intervention designs, I analyze developmental trajectories using latent growth modeling, sequence analysis, and network-based interaction modeling. With over 10,000 learner–AI interaction texts collected across naturalistic and experimental settings, my work focuses on understanding how external scaffolds become internalized cognitive strategies.

I am particularly interested in identifying critical developmental inflection points — such as mid-trajectory plateaus — where targeted intervention can promote sustained growth. My research integrates classroom-based ecological validity with advanced quantitative modeling to inform developmentally grounded learning interventions.
